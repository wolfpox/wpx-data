## Notes for authoring

- Keep `education` and `why` fields in the same neutral tone as existing
  entries — concrete, calm, never alarmist.
- Cadence frequencies must be drawn from the buckets in `SCHEMA.md`.
- When an item naturally pairs with another (e.g. furnace + thermostat),
  add it to the other entry's `related_types`.
- Custom-built items by users will use this same shape with most fields
  blank, so don't invent extra fields here.
